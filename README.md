## About Pollaris

**Pollaris** (a blend of *Poll* and *Polaris* — the North Star) is a community-driven decision-support web application designed to help users navigate everyday dilemmas and make informed choices.

### Problem & Mission
When seeking advice on social media, opinions are often scattered across long comment threads, unstructured, and difficult to evaluate. Pollaris addresses this challenge by providing an organized, interactive platform where users can gather diverse community perspectives, visualize consensus, and find the right direction when faced with tough decisions.

### Key Features
- **Poll Creation & Secure Voting:** Create polls with customizable options, image attachments, and expiration countdowns; cast votes with strict concurrency control and real-time visualization.
- **In-depth Community Discussions:** Share arguments, reply in threads, and tag comments with specific options to provide clear context.
- **AI Opinion Summary:** Analyzes community feedback on demand to generate grounded summaries highlighting prevailing sentiments and a structured pros & cons breakdown for each option.
- **AI Decision Assistant:** Delivers tailored, objective recommendations by cross-referencing individual constraints (budget, preferences, time limits) with community insights.
- **Search, Discovery & Result Sharing:** Filter polls by category and popularity, bookmark favorite polls, and generate shareable decision cards for social media.
